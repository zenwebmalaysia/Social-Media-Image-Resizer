Tips

  • Portrait wins on phones. 4:5 for Instagram and Facebook feeds and 3:4 for Xiaohongshu take more screen space than square or landscape.
  • Keep text away from the edges of Stories, Reels and TikTok – buttons and captions cover the top and bottom.
  • Start from the largest, best-quality photo you have. Small images stretched up look blurry.
  • Use “Fit with border” for logos, product shots and posters where nothing may be cut off.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The tool uses your browser to resize the image on your own device. Nothing is sent to ZenWeb or anyone else.

Why does my image look blurry after posting?

Usually the original was too small, or the platform compressed it. Start with a large photo and use the sizes here. For images with text or logos, PNG often stays sharper.

Which size should I use for Facebook and Instagram ads?

Use 1080 × 1350 (4:5) for feeds and 1080 × 1920 (9:16) for Stories and Reels. Uploading both lets Meta show the best fit in each placement.

What about Xiaohongshu?

3:4 portrait (1080 × 1440) is the most common note size and fills the feed card well.
