# Social Media Image Resizer

Upload one image and get it resized and cropped for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Xiaohongshu, LinkedIn, YouTube, X, Google Business Profile and website share images – all in your browser.

**Use it online:** https://zenwebmalaysia.github.io/social-image-resizer/

Built by [ZenWeb](https://zenweb.my/), a digital marketing agency in Petaling Jaya, Malaysia (SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, web design and development).

## Features

- 14 common sizes, including Instagram 4:5 and 9:16, Facebook link images, **Xiaohongshu 3:4 (1080 × 1440)**, LinkedIn banner and YouTube thumbnail.
- **Crop to fill** with a choice of which part to keep (9 focus points), or **Fit with border** in any colour – useful for logos and posters.
- JPG or PNG output, download one by one or all at once.
- Warns when the original is smaller than the output size.
- Includes a size cheat sheet.

Sizes are the commonly recommended ones as of 2026 – platforms change them from time to time.

## Privacy

Images are processed with the browser's canvas and **never uploaded** anywhere.
